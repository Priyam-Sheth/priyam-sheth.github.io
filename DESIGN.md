# Design (v3, 4 Sep 2026): "Daylight studio"

Painted panels, one light serif with italic emphasis, mono chapter marks, and product windows
you can watch work. The language is borrowed from heylemon.ai (the reference Priyam chose);
palette, painted assets, objects and copy are our own. Files: `index.html`, `src/v3/site.css`,
`src/v3/site.js`, `assets/paint/*.webp` (generated by `scripts/paint.py`). Reasoning in
`docs/v2-plan.md` (v2 plan plus the v3 addendum).

## Theme
Light. Ground `#F5F4EF` (warm stone, low chroma), ink `#16181D`. Colour lives in the painted
plates, not the page ground. One dark plate (numbers) and a dark footer.

## Color
- `--bg` #F5F4EF · `--bg-2` #ECEBE4 · `--card` #FFFFFF
- `--ink` #16181D · `--ink-2` #3A3F48 · `--ink-3` #6B717C · lines at 10% and 18% ink
- `--teal` #0F766E / `--teal-2` #0B5C56 (marks, results) · `--teal-lit` #5FE0CF on night
- `--mari` #FFD84D marigold: the highlighter in quotes, the accent inside dark objects
- `--coral` #E8613C only for "short / unknown" states in demos
- Night plate: `#0C1A20` ground, text `#EAF1F2`, dim `#9FB3BB`
- Paintings: dusk (apricot to teal, hero and two panels), gold (sage to gold, VS), rose (rose
  to lavender, layers, panels, close), night (teal-black, numbers). 1600×1000 webp, ~110 KB each.

## Typography
- Display: Sentient 400 (italic 400 for the emphasised phrase in every heading). 300 available.
- Body and UI: General Sans 400/500/600.
- Mono: Fragment Mono 400 for chapter marks, fine print, machine text (the say-bar, the
  "you do" cards, table cells, scan codes).
- Scale: hero clamp(2.7rem, 6.2vw, 5.4rem); h2 clamp(2.3rem, 4.4vw, 3.6rem); panel h3
  clamp(1.6rem, 2.6vw, 2.2rem); body 17px; small 15px; mono 12px.
- Every heading has exactly one italic phrase. Marks are uppercase mono with a dot each side,
  at the top of a plate or section, never twice in a section.

## Shape and depth
- Plates 30px radius, cards 22px, app windows 16px, pills full.
- Window shadow: three layers, deepest 0 50px 90px at 10% ink. Card shadow lighter.
- Dark objects (say-bar, "you do" cards, VS system side, dark quote card) are the ink colour
  with marigold labels.

## Components
- **Plate**: painted background via `::before` with a slow vertical drift on a view timeline.
- **Say-bar + window**: the Lemon device. Dark mono pill riding the top edge of a white app
  window with traffic lights, a title and a mono meta label.
- **Chat simulation** (hero): three chips, scripted exchanges, typing indicator, hidden amounts
  as blurred pills. Fixed 20rem height, scrolls inside. Labelled "simulation · real format".
- **Steps**: three cards with painted tops holding one real object each (a locked decision, the
  green gate, a Telegram alert), numbered 01 to 03 because they are a real sequence.
- **Tabs + stage**: six tabs switch panels (Live Commerce has the night painting); the stage's painting changes with the panel.
  Each panel: mark, h3, lede, then "you do" card → arrow → app window, then a result line.
- **Strip**: a scroll-snap gallery of framed screenshots with captions and arrow buttons, on a
  translucent tray so captions read on any painting. Used on the Wolf Eyes and Live Commerce panels.
- **Phones**: two phone frames side by side inside an app window (Live Commerce: stream + sheet).
- **Facts**: a three-column grid of measured claims, serif value with one italic word, mono-free.
- **VS**: two cards, by hand vs with the system, with bars that grow on entry.
- **Screen**: a framed area with two pill marquees (the only marquees on the page).
- **Stats**: four cells on the night plate, counters tick up once.
- **Love**: one big marigold-tinted quote, one dark call card, three small cards.
- **Close**: rose plate, two buttons, copy-email.

## Motion
- Easing `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`. Reveals 800ms with 60ms steps. Pop-in for messages 320ms.
- Bars (the "listening" glyph) loop at 900ms; cursor blink 1s; marquees 56s and 62s linear.
- Only transform, opacity, clip-path animate. Fonts use `font-display: block` so the large
  serif does not reflow (CLS measured 0.013 desktop, 0.001 mobile).
- Reduced motion: no reveals, no drift, no marquee (pills wrap), bars still, demos jump to
  their final state, slider does not nudge.

## Verification
`node scripts/verify.mjs <outdir> [desk|mob|rm|nojs|lap]` renders every section and each tab,
and reports console errors, 404s, horizontal overflow and CLS. `node scripts/og/build-og.mjs`
rebuilds the share image. Ship only when all passes report none / 0 / < 0.02.
