Inventory Reconciliation ERP

Government lens orders, matched against stock

Palak Enterprise · 4 of 5

Palak Optical supplies ophthalmic lenses against government orders. Matching each order line to what is actually on the shelf was done by hand in Excel, every day. I built the system that does the matching, including the part a spreadsheet cannot do: finding a different lens that still works for the patient.

What I built

  • A two-pass allocation engine: exact stock matches first, substitutes second
  • Substitute matching that finds clinically acceptable lenses within a 0.25 dioptre tolerance
  • Every order line reconciled against live stock, no spreadsheet copy-paste
  • Results the buyer can act on directly: what is allocated, what is substituted, what is short

Two to three hours of manual Excel matching every day now finishes in under 30 seconds, and about 15% of stock was saved that would have been re-ordered unnecessarily.

Live in production · results signed off by the client

Unified Inventory Control System

Real-time barcode stock audit

Palak Enterprise · 5 of 5

A full physical stock audit used to mean staff counting on separate sheets and arguing about the totals afterwards. UICS lets several staff scan at the same time against one shared live count. The SQL behind it sorts what they scan into expected stock, excess stock and unknown stock, so the audit ends with three answers the owner can act on instead of one discrepancy column nobody can use.

What I built

  • Concurrent scanning: several staff, one shared live count, updating in real time
  • Custom SQL that separates expected, excess and unknown stock
  • Barcode scanners on the floor, with the count visible while it happens
  • An audit that closes with a clean result, not a pile of unexplained differences

A full physical stock audit that took about 3 days now takes about 4 hours.

Live in production · result signed off by the client
