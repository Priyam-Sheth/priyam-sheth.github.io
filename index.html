About

Who is behind it

I am Priyam Sheth, an independent AI systems builder. I ship production software for real businesses: B2B platforms, AI employees trained on a company's own data, and the unglamorous automation underneath. I work in three layers at once: product judgement, architecture, and the code itself.

Two threads run through the work. Deterministic engineering, where the domain math has to be exactly right: settlement cycles, allocation rules, substitution tolerances. And agentic systems, where an AI does the daily work with memory, guardrails and receipts. The first proves the second is real engineering, not glue.

I also built Donna, my own AI: permanent memory, corrects herself when facts change, and checks every reply against a record of what actually ran.
