Inventory Reconciliation ERP

Government lens orders, matched against stock · Palak Optical

Live in production · results signed off by the client

Palak Optical supplies ophthalmic lenses against government orders. Matching each order line to what is actually on the shelf was done by hand in Excel, every day. I built the system that does the matching, including the part a spreadsheet cannot do: finding a different lens that still works for the patient.

  • 2 to 3 h → 30 sdaily matching, by hand before, now under 30 seconds
  • ~15%of stock saved that would have been re-ordered unnecessarily
  • Two-pass allocation: exact stock matches first, then clinically acceptable substitutes within 0.25 dioptre
  • Reads the government's messy order files as they come; rejects what it cannot read with confidence rather than mis-allocating
  • Ends with three lists the buyer can act on: allocated, substituted, short

Unified Inventory Control System

Real-time barcode stock audit · Palak Enterprise

Live in production · result signed off by the client

0Expectedon the shelf, in the books
0Excessmore on the shelf than recorded
0Unknownon the shelf, not in the books at all
Scanning …0 scanned · example run

A full physical stock audit used to mean staff counting on separate sheets and arguing about the totals afterwards. UICS lets several staff scan at the same time against one shared live count, and sorts every scan into expected, excess or unknown stock, so the audit ends with three answers the owner can act on instead of one discrepancy column nobody can use.

  • 3 days → 4 hoursa full physical stock audit, before and after
  • Concurrent scanning: phones on the floor, a desktop at the master station, one live count
  • Blind scanning: the desktop detects the barcode gun's keystroke speed, so no field needs focus
  • Custom SQL that separates expected, excess and unknown, live, as scans arrive
